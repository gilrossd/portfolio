# Company Risk and Financial Performance Analysis

1. Project Overview:
The objective of this project was to analyze financial data 
and identify patterns in revenue, company performance, 
and year-over-year growth.

The project follows an end-to-end financial analysis project using Excel, MySQL, and Power BI.

The dataset was synthetic provided in Excel format and was prepared for
analysis using Excel, MySQL, and Power BI.

2. Data Quality Assessment

Before analysis, the dataset was reviewed for common data quality
issues.

The following issues were identified:

- Missing values
- Inconsistent date formats
- Inconsistent data types
- Negative financial values
- Duplicate records
- Blank fields
- Inconsistent company names

These issues were reviewed and addressed during the data-cleaning
process to improve consistency and reliability of the analysis.

3. Data Cleaning

The following cleaning steps were performed:

1. Checked for duplicate records.
2. Standardized date formats.
3. Identified and reviewed missing values.
4. Corrected inconsistent data types.
5. Standardized company names and industry
6. Prepared the cleaned dataset for MySQL analysis and Power BI.


4. Tools I used.
- Excel for data cleaning
- MySQL for analyzation
- Power BI for visualization

5. Analysis
- Revenue analysis
- Company contribution
- Year-over-year revenue changes
- Financial trends
- Company risk analysis

6. Business Questions
- Which companies generate the most total revenue?
- What percentage of total revenue does each company contribute?
- How does revenue change year over year?
- Which companies show significant changes in revenue?
- What are the major financial trends?

7. Business Insights
- Total revenue across the dataset was approximately $116.98M,
  with total operating income of approximately $32.70M.
- Revenue remained relatively stable from 2022 to 2025. Revenue declined by approximately 2.82% in 2024
  before recovering by approximately 1.16% in 2025.
- Apex Retail generated the highest total revenue at approximately $14.38M.
- Insight Media recorded the highest average profit margin at approximately 30.10%,
  while Kite Travel had the lowest at approximately 24.68%.
- The average debt-to-assets ratio was approximately 41.09%,
  with differences in financial leverage across companies.
- Approximately 57.17% of transactions were classified as low risk,
  24.08% as medium risk, and 18.75% as high risk.
- Europe had the highest proportion of high-risk observations at 20.57%,
  while Latin America had the lowest at 14.40% but the highest proportion of medium-risk observations at 30.40%.
