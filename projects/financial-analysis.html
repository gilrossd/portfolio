PROJECT 01

Company Risk and Financial Performance Analysis

An end-to-end data analysis project using Excel, MySQL, and Power BI to explore financial performance, revenue trends, company contribution, and year-over-year changes.

Tools Excel, MySQL, Power BI
Focus Financial Analysis
Skills Cleaning, SQL, Visualization

Project Overview

The objective of this project was to analyze financial data and identify patterns in revenue, company performance, and year-over-year growth.

The project follows an end-to-end workflow: data cleaning, exploratory analysis, SQL analysis, and dashboard development.

Business Questions

  • Which companies generate the most total revenue?
  • What percentage of total revenue does each company contribute?
  • How does revenue change year over year?
  • Which companies show significant changes in revenue?
  • What are the major financial trends?

Data Cleaning

The raw dataset was reviewed and standardized before analysis.

  • Checked for missing values.
  • Removed or investigated duplicate records.
  • Standardized company names.
  • Cleaned and standardized date fields.
  • Checked numerical columns for consistency.
  • Reviewed unusual or invalid values.

Excel and SQL for Analysis

Excel and MySQL was used to answer business questions and calculate financial metrics.

  • Pivot Table to summarize reports
  • Aggregations using SUM and AVG.
  • GROUP BY and ORDER BY.
  • Common Table Expressions.
  • Window functions.
  • Subqueries for revenue contribution.
  • LAG() for year-over-year analysis.

Power BI Dashboard

The final analysis was visualized in Power BI to make financial trends and company performance easier to understand.

The dashboard includes financial KPIs, company comparisons over revenue and profit, risk level and year-over-year analysis.

Business Insights

The analysis showed that company performance varied depending on the financial metric being evaluated. Revenue, profitability, and risk indicators were analyzed to provide a broader view of company performance.

  • Total revenue across the dataset was approximately $116.98M, with total operating income of approximately $32.70M.
  • Revenue remained relatively stable from 2022 to 2025. Revenue declined by approximately 2.82% in 2024 before recovering by approximately 1.16% in 2025.
  • Apex Retail generated the highest total revenue at approximately $14.38M.
  • Insight Media recorded the highest average profit margin at approximately 30.10%, while Kite Travel had the lowest at approximately 24.68%.
  • The average debt-to-assets ratio was approximately 41.09%, with differences in financial leverage across companies.
  • Approximately 57.17% of transactions were classified as low risk, 24.08% as medium risk, and 18.75% as high risk.
  • Europe had the highest proportion of high-risk observations at 20.57%, while Latin America had the lowest at 14.40% but the highest proportion of medium-risk observations at 30.40%.
