EXCEL DATA ANALYSIS PROJECT

Bike Buyers Customer Analysis

Excel  •  PivotTables  •  Nested IFS  •  Interactive Dashboard

An exploratory analysis analyzing customer demographic factors—including income levels, age brackets, and commute distances—to evaluate purchasing behavior and optimize targeted marketing strategies for bike sales.

← Back to Projects View on GitHub

OVERVIEW

Project Background

Understanding customer behavior is essential for optimizing product offerings and targeting advertising effectively. This project examines a dataset of 1,000 potential customers to analyze how demographic variables such as age, income level, and daily commute distance influence the decision to purchase a bicycle.

WORKFLOW & METHODOLOGY

Data Cleaning & Transformation

  • Data Standardisation & Cleaning: Processed raw demographic records by eliminating duplicate rows, standardizing categorical text fields (e.g., converting "M" and "S" to "Married" and "Single", "F" and "M" to "Female" and "Male"), and handling missing entries.
  • Feature Engineering: Applied custom IFS / NESTED IFS formulas to create dynamic age brackets (Adolescent, Middle Age, and Old) to enable granular cohort analysis.
  • Pivot Table Aggregations: Structured PivotTables to summarize average income per purchase outcome, commute distance distribution, and purchase conversion rates across distinct age groups.
  • Dynamic Dashboard Design: Built an interactive Excel dashboard complete with charts and interactive Slicers (filtered by Region, Education Level, and Marital Status) for dynamic insights exploration.

INSIGHTS

Key Analytical Findings

1. Income Level vs. Purchase Decision

Higher average income directly correlates with bike purchases across both male and female demographics. Customers who purchased a bike had an average income significantly higher than non-buyers.

2. Commute Distance Impact

Customers residing within 0–1 miles of their workplace demonstrated the highest purchase conversion rate, whereas motivation dropped significantly for commutes exceeding 5 miles.

3. Age Demographic Trends

The Middle Age demographic (31–54 years old) represents the largest proportion of bike buyers, making them the primary target audience for sales campaigns.

DASHBOARD

Interactive Excel Dashboard

The final interactive dashboard allows users to slice data by geographic region, marital status, and educational background to explore custom customer segments dynamically.
